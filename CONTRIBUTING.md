# Contributing

This club follows the
[Ishraqa contributing guide](https://github.com/ishraqa-app/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
and the [code of conduct](https://github.com/ishraqa-app/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

In short: open or claim an issue, work on a branch, open a pull request, and wait for a review.
Club leads may add project-specific rules below.
